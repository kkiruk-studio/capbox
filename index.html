HOW IT WORKS01–03

One scan, one tap, done.

01SCAN

Scan your camera roll

CapBox sweeps your photo library and shows you exactly how many screenshots are still waiting to be sorted.

02SORT

Confirm the AI suggestion

On-device AI suggests a folder for each screenshot — sort one at a time, or select a batch and send them all at once.
